FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Send us a message and we’ll come back to you.

What is energy invoice validation?

Energy invoice validation is the line-by-line checking of an electricity or gas bill against the supply contract, meter data and previous billing records to identify charges that may be incorrect, missing or unexpected.

Do you validate both electricity and gas invoices?

Yes. We review UK electricity (MPAN) and gas (MPRN) invoices, including unit rates, standing charges, consumption, non-commodity charges, CCL and VAT.

What information do you need from us?

Ideally the invoices, the signed contract or price confirmation, and any available meter reads or half-hourly data. We can still check arithmetic, VAT, CCL and consistency where contract information is limited, and we will note any gaps in the report.

Do you guarantee savings or refunds?

No. We identify potential discrepancies based on the information supplied. Any findings should be confirmed with the relevant supplier or an authorised professional.

Can you handle recurring monthly volumes?

Yes. We support one-off reviews, historical reconciliations and recurring monthly workloads for consultants, brokers, suppliers and businesses.

How is our data kept confidential?

Invoices and records are exchanged through an agreed secure channel, used only for the agreed scope of work and handled confidentially.
