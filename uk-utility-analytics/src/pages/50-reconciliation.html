What we reconcile

Reconciliation answers one question for every supply and period: has it been billed once, correctly, and are any credits fully accounted for?

Credit notes

Each credit note matched to the invoice it reverses, for the full amount, including VAT.

Rebills

Reissued invoices checked for the corrected rates, readings or periods, and the net effect calculated.

Billing period coverage

Every period billed once: no gaps, duplicates or overlaps across the invoice history.

Invoice totals and VAT

Line calculations, VAT rates and totals recalculated for each invoice in scope.

Historical invoice reconciliation

Rates, consumption and charges reviewed across months, so patterns stand out, not just single errors.

Statements and payments

Where you provide an account statement, invoices and credits compared with it.

FAQ

Reconciliation questions

How far back can a historical review go?

As far back as you can supply invoices and supporting records. We agree the period with you when we set the scope.

What happens when a supplier issues a rebill?

The original invoice should be credited in full and a corrected invoice issued. We check both documents and confirm the period has been billed once, at the right figures.

Can reconciliation be combined with other checks?

Yes. It is often combined with contract rate verification and meter data checks for a complete review.
