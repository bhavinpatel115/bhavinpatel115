About us

Smarter energy billing. Greater accuracy.

UK Utility Analytics provides specialist back-office support for UK energy billing operations. We focus on detailed invoice checking, contract comparisons, billing data analysis and structured reporting, so your team can review energy invoices efficiently and concentrate on the items that need further investigation.

We support UK businesses and energy professionals through flexible service arrangements, from a single invoice to a recurring monthly workload.

  1. An energy invoice is dozens of charges, not one

    Unit rates, standing charges, capacity, network and non-commodity charges, levies, VAT and adjustments. Each line can be wrong in its own way.

  2. The figures come from several sources

    Contract prices, meter readings, consumption estimates and pass-through network costs all feed the bill. A mismatch in any one of them reaches the total.

  3. Unchecked errors tend to repeat

    A wrong rate or meter detail set up once is often billed again every month until someone compares the invoice with the records.

FAQ

Energy invoice validation questions

Something not covered here? Send us your question.

What is energy invoice validation?

It is the line-by-line checking of an electricity or gas invoice against the supply contract, meter data and previous billing records, to identify charges that may be incorrect, missing or unexpected. It is also called energy bill validation.

Do you check both electricity and gas invoices?

Yes. We review electricity invoices (identified by MPAN) and gas invoices (identified by MPRN), including rates, standing charges, consumption, non-commodity charges, CCL and VAT. See electricity bill checking and gas invoice validation.

What information do you need from us?

Ideally the invoices, the contract or price confirmation, and any meter readings or half-hourly data you hold. Where contract information is limited we can still check calculations, VAT, CCL and consistency, and the report will note what could not be verified.

Do you guarantee savings or refunds?

No. We identify potential discrepancies based on the information supplied. Findings should be confirmed with the relevant supplier or an authorised professional.

Do you raise queries with suppliers?

Your team decides which items to raise. We prepare the supporting detail for each supplier query so it can be sent with the evidence attached.

Can you handle recurring monthly volumes?

Yes. We support one-off reviews, historical reconciliations and recurring monthly workloads.

How is our information kept confidential?

Invoices and records are exchanged through an agreed secure channel, used only for the agreed scope of work and handled confidentially.
