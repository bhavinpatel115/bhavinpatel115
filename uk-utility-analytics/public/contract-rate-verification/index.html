Contract rate verification

We compare the unit rates, standing charges and contract terms on each energy invoice with the documentation you supply. Rates that do not match, changes applied on the wrong date and supplies billed outside their contract are all reported with evidence.

What we verify

A contract sets the prices. The invoice should follow them exactly, for every supply and every day of the billing period.

Unit rates

Each rate band on the invoice, such as day, night or single rate, compared with the price confirmation.

Standing charges

The daily standing charge and the number of days billed.

Contract dates

Contract start and end dates, and any price change applied from the correct date, including split billing periods.

Supplies covered

Every MPAN or MPRN on the contract billed at contract rates, and no supply billed that is not yours.

Out-of-contract rates

Supplies whose fixed contract has ended without a new one in place, which are often billed at higher out-of-contract prices.

Deemed rates

Supplies taken over without a contract, for example after moving into premises, which are billed at deemed rates until a contract is agreed.

What we need from you

  • The contract, price confirmation or tender schedule for each supply
  • The invoices for the period to be reviewed
  • A list of supplies (MPANs and MPRNs) and sites, if you have one

No contract to hand?

We can still compare rates across consecutive invoices, identify unexplained price changes and flag supplies that appear to be on out-of-contract or deemed terms. The report will state clearly which findings could not be verified against a contract.

FAQ

Contract rate questions

What is the difference between deemed and out-of-contract rates?

Out-of-contract rates apply when a fixed contract ends and no new contract has been agreed. Deemed rates apply when a business uses energy at premises without having agreed a contract, for example after moving in. Both are usually higher than negotiated contract prices.

Do you check both electricity and gas contracts?

Yes. Contract rate verification covers electricity and gas, and can be combined with full electricity bill checking or gas invoice validation.

Can you review rates across many sites at once?

Yes. For multi-site portfolios we check every supply on the contract schedule and report exceptions by site and supply.
