Electricity invoice validation

Business electricity bill checking

We check UK business electricity invoices line by line against the contract, meter data and billing history you supply. Each potential error, inconsistency, missing item or unexpected charge is reported with its evidence and a recommended next step.

What we check on a business electricity invoice

A business electricity bill combines energy costs, network costs, levies and tax. We review each part separately, then confirm the invoice adds up.

Supply and invoice details

The MPAN, meter serial number, site address and billing period match your records, with no gaps or overlaps between invoices.

Unit rates and standing charges

Each rate band and the daily standing charge compared with the contract terms you supply. See contract rate verification.

Consumption and meter readings

Billed kWh reconciled to the readings or half-hourly data, with estimated reads identified. See MPAN & MPRN checks.

Capacity and excess capacity

For half-hourly supplies, capacity (kVA) charges checked against the agreed supply capacity, and any excess capacity charges reviewed.

Reactive power charges

Reactive charges identified and reviewed against the metered data available.

Transmission, distribution and network charges

Non-commodity lines such as transmission and distribution use of system charges reviewed for consistency with the contract structure and supporting information.

Climate Change Levy

CCL applied at the right rate, and any relief or exemption you hold reflected on the invoice.

VAT and invoice arithmetic

The VAT rate applied, line calculations and the invoice total checked.

Common issues we look for

  • Rates that differ from the price confirmation, or a rate change applied on the wrong date
  • A supply billed at out-of-contract or deemed rates while a contract is in place
  • Repeated estimated readings, or a catch-up bill after a long run of estimates
  • Capacity charges based on a different kVA figure from the one agreed
  • CCL charged where a relief or exemption has been notified
  • Duplicate or overlapping billing periods

What we need from you

  • The electricity invoices to be checked, as PDFs or data files
  • The contract or price confirmation for each supply
  • Meter readings or half-hourly data, where you have them
  • Any CCL or VAT certificates relevant to the sites

Missing something? We can still check the calculations and consistency, and the report will state what could not be verified.

FAQ

Electricity bill checking questions

Do you check half-hourly and non-half-hourly supplies?

Yes. Half-hourly invoices usually carry more line items, such as capacity and time-banded network charges, so we review those against the half-hourly data and contract. Non-half-hourly invoices are checked against meter readings and the contract rates.

Can you check invoices on pass-through contracts?

Yes. Where non-commodity charges are passed through and itemised on the invoice, we review each item against the supporting information available and flag anything that looks inconsistent.

Is electricity bill checking the same as bill validation?

Yes. Bill checking, bill validation and invoice validation all describe comparing an invoice with the contract, meter data and billing records to find potential errors.
